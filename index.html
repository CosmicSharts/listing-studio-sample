Part one

Listing videos

01

Walkthrough movement from still photos

Each photo becomes a few seconds of moving camera. Near furniture slides against the far windows and the view, the way it does when someone walks a gimbal through the room, so the reel reads as a filmed walkthrough.

How it is made

Your photo is the first frame. An AI video model continues it into a short, slow camera move, and the shots are cut together with room names, key facts, music and your details.

Where to use it

  • The main reel on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
  • WhatsApp to a buyer who asks "do you have a video?"
  • The video slot on portal listings.

Why it helps

  • Movement holds attention longer than a slideshow.
  • No shoot to book, no access to arrange with a tenant, no waiting for an edit.

What gets checked

Every moving shot is compared with its photo before it goes in. If the movement invents or changes anything in the room or the view, that shot is replaced with a plain camera pan across the real photo. An advert must only show what is there.

New · Location film

From the map to the balcony, with the sun worked out

One continuous film that answers three questions a buyer asks before they agree to a viewing: where is it, what is the building like, and when does the balcony get the sun.

  • The map: a real map of the area with the distance to each landmark, then a zoom down to the building and an arrow for the way the balcony faces.
  • Street level: a look around from outside, with the whole building in view.
  • The climb: up the front of the tower to the apartment's floor.
  • The balcony: the day played through from sunrise to sunset, with the time direct sun arrives and where the sun sets in the view.

How the sun is worked out

The sun's position is calculated for the building's location, the date and each minute of the day, then placed in the balcony picture from the balcony's compass bearing. You give the bearing, or it is read from the floor plan and the map.

Why it helps

  • "Is it morning or afternoon sun?" is answered before the buyer asks.
  • A sunset-facing balcony becomes something you can show, not only claim.
  • Overseas buyers see the location, the building and the outlook in thirty seconds.

What gets checked

The map and the sun times are real calculations. The sun estimate does not account for neighbouring buildings, and the film says so on screen. In this sample the address, the bearing, the street view, the climb and the balcony pictures are invented; a real one uses your balcony photo and the real bearing.

Part three

Image services

Make every photo the best version of the room

  • Virtual staging: an empty room shown furnished, in a style chosen for the likely buyer.
  • Declutter: a lived-in or tenanted home shown tidy, without asking anyone to move their things.
  • Day to dusk: a daytime exterior shown at twilight with the lights on.

Also available, not shown here

  • Straighten, brighten and colour-correct phone photos.
  • Replace a grey sky with a clear one.
  • Blur faces, family photos, number plates and paperwork for privacy.
  • Redraw a scanned floor plan cleanly, with sizes.

Why it helps

Buyers struggle to picture an empty room, tenanted homes rarely photograph well, and twilight exteriors are the images that get clicked. Staging a room digitally costs a fraction of furnishing it.

What gets checked

Only furniture, clutter or light may change. Walls, windows, floor and view must stay exactly as photographed, and every edited picture carries a line saying so.

Questions

Things agents usually ask

Do you need to visit the property?

No. Everything is built from the photos and details you already have for the listing.

What photos work best?

The normal wide listing photos, as large as you have them. One per room, plus the view and the building. Phone photos work if they are level and well lit, and they can be corrected.

Does it work for villas, off-plan and rentals?

Yes. Villas get more rooms and outdoor shots. Off-plan uses the developer's approved renders and adds the payment plan. Rentals usually take a shorter pack.

Is it honest to use AI on a property advert?

The rule here is that the property must never be changed. Movement is checked shot by shot against the photo, staged and relit pictures are labelled on screen, and anything that fails the check falls back to the untouched photo.

Can it carry my brokerage's look?

Yes. Colours, typefaces, logo, your name and number are set once and reused on every piece. The end card has a place for the permit number and its QR code.

What about music?

Each video comes with an original instrumental track, so there is nothing to clear. You can still swap in a trending sound inside Instagram or TikTok when you post.

Which languages?

English, Arabic, Russian and Mandarin are shown here. Others can be added to suit your buyers.

Can I change things after I see it?

Yes. Wording, the order of rooms, which shots move, and the figures shown are all quick to change.
